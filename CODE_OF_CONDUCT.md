# Code of Conduct

mcp-proton is an independent project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Proton AG.

This summary adopts the principles of the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1. The full text is at <https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/>. Where this summary and the full text differ, the full text applies.

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone. This applies regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that supports a healthy community:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language.
- Respecting differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Accepting constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focusing on what is best for the community and the users.
- Showing empathy toward other community members.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information, such as addresses or message contents, without explicit permission.
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

Security reports and reports of private mail data in public places are handled under [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) and must not be posted publicly.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies in project spaces, and in public spaces when a person represents the project.

## Enforcement

Enforcement contact: to be set by the maintainers when the repository is published.

Maintainers review reports and decide on a response. Responses can include a private warning, a request for public apology, a temporary ban or a permanent ban from project spaces.
